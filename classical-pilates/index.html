What classical means

The original system, in its original order

Classical Pilates follows the exercises, sequence and apparatus Joseph Pilates developed, passed on teacher to teacher. Nina teaches in a direct line from Joseph Pilates through Romana Kryzanowska.

The work moves between the reformer, Cadillac, Wunda chair, ladder barrel and mat, so every session builds strength, mobility and control across the whole body.

What guides the work

Three principles

01

Precision over intensity

Doing less, correctly, produces more lasting change than doing more with poor form. Every repetition is intentional.

02

The body as a system

A stiff shoulder isn’t always a shoulder problem. Posture, breathing and alignment are read as a whole before anything is isolated.

03

Longevity as the metric

The question is always whether this will serve you in ten or twenty years. The practice should compound over time.
