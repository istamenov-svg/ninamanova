Two age groups

Built for how kids grow

6–11

Play-based movement

Shorter, playful sessions that build coordination, balance and body awareness, using small props and simple Pilates foundations children can enjoy.

12–17

Strength and posture

Core strength, posture for long school days and screens, mobility, and balanced strength that supports the sports they already play.

Formats

Three ways to train

01

Private

One child, one teacher. The session is built entirely around your child’s age, goals and pace.

02

Siblings or friends

Two or three children together. Shared motivation, with each child cued individually.

03

Parent and child

Train together. A shared hour of movement, and habits that carry on at home.

In your home, your building gym or the studio. Pricing is tailored to format and location and shared at your consultation.

How it works

Parents stay in the loop

  • A parent or guardian books every session and joins the free consultation
  • A parent or guardian stays at home during in-home sessions
  • Sessions for children who are injured, or who have a medical condition, start only with their doctor’s clearance
  • Short updates after sessions on what your child worked on
