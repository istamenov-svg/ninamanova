What you eat is part of how you move

Digestion, sleep and energy all connect back to your plate. Nourishment is guidance you can add to your Pilates sessions: practical, evidence-based, and built around the way you already live.

Add Nourishment to your sessions →
What opting in adds

Guidance, not a diet

No meal plans and no diets. Simple, lasting changes that support the work you do on the apparatus.

01

An intake

A short conversation at the start about how you eat, sleep and recover, and what you want to change.

02

Check-ins

Brief check-ins within your sessions: what’s working, what isn’t, and one small next step.

03

Practical ideas

Food, hydration and recovery ideas that fit your preferences, culture and schedule.

A simple framework

Ideas that hold up, in research and in practice

01

Eat whole, mostly plants

Vegetables, fruit, legumes, nuts and seeds form a durable foundation that has survived every fad cycle.

02

Support recovery

Adequate protein, anti-inflammatory foods and hydration are basic maintenance for a body that works hard.

03

Reduce the noise

Most confusion comes from too much advice, not too much food. Eat real food, notice how it makes you feel, adjust.

Credentials

Grounded in nutrition science

Nina completed Stanford’s Nutrition Science program and Vanderbilt’s Nutrition, Health & Lifestyle course in 2024, and the Food Revolution Network’s Plant-Based Coaching Masterclass.

Nourishment guidance is general wellness information, not medical nutrition therapy. If you are under a physician’s care for a condition, please follow their advice.
