Building gyms

In your building gym

Many condominiums along the Intracoastal and the beach have a fitness room. With your building's permission, sessions can take place there, and if the gym has a reformer, sessions can use it.

Semi-private sessions work well for couples, friends or neighbors in the same building: two people, each cued individually.

Why at home

Fewer obstacles, more consistency

  • No commute, parking or class schedule to work around
  • Complete privacy and one-to-one attention
  • Easier to keep two sessions a week, which is where results come from
  • Family members can join as semi-private or parent-and-child sessions

Questions

In-home Pilates FAQ

What equipment do I need at home?

None. Nina brings a mat and small props. A clear space about the size of a yoga mat plus an arm's length on each side is enough.

Can sessions take place in my building gym?

Yes, with your building's permission. If the gym has a reformer, sessions can use it.

Which areas do you cover?

Coral Ridge, The Landings, Bay Colony, Sea Ranch Lakes, Lauderdale-by-the-Sea and nearby Fort Lauderdale, including zip codes 33301, 33304, 33305, 33306, 33308, 33316, 33062 and 33064. Outside these areas, ask: online sessions are always an option.

How long and how often are sessions?

Every session is one hour. How often you train is agreed at the free consultation; two sessions a week is the usual starting point for steady progress.

What is your cancellation policy?

Sessions can be rescheduled at no charge with at least 24 hours' notice. Cancellations or no-shows within 24 hours forfeit that session.
